About Ryan Morris

Ryan Morris is a Niagara Region stand-up comedian who documents comedy the way a programmer documents code: every set numbered, recorded, and reviewed. By day he works in tech; by night he bombs and kills on purpose — building material through volume at rooms like Laugh Lab / Bomb Comedy Tour, Spotlight Comedy Club, and open mics across Southern Ontario.

His stage persona leans observational and self-aware: dad life, cat jokes, topical bits, puppets, props, and unhinged crowd work. Recurring character energy includes Buffalo Fan Bill. The brand is COMEDY.EXE — authenticity over algorithm chasing.

Day job / night mic

Systems and data by day (see github.com/hackmods). Stand-up craft logs by night. Same iteration loop — ship, review, improve.
