About Ryan Morris

“I don’t repeat jokes, I repeat mistakes.” Ryan Morris is a Niagara Falls stand-up comedian known for unhinged crowd work, experimental anti-comedy, and the absolute worst dad-life advice on the internet. He numbers, films, and reviews every set — 139 and counting — the way a programmer ships code.

What you get on stage comes in three flavours: crowd work disasters (front-row roasts, hecklers, rooms going off the rails), dad-life rants and bad advice (Half Christmas, PTA meetings, French immersion), and props that shouldn’t be there — puppets, keyboards, paper bags, a ketchup packet. He’s a Monday regular at The Bomb Comedy Show in Niagara Falls, a co-host of The Green Room podcast, and performs in virtual reality at Failed To Render VR. Recurring characters include Buffalo Fan Bill and Gary the puppet.

Day job / night mic

Systems and data by day (see github.com/hackmods). Stand-up craft logs by night. Same iteration loop — ship, review, improve.

Long (press / website)

Ryan Morris doesn’t repeat jokes — he repeats mistakes. A Niagara Falls stand-up comedian with a tech day job, he treats stage time like shipping code: number the set, film it, review it, ship the next one. The result is a growing archive of raw Canadian indie comedy built on high-friction crowd work, existential rants about surviving parenthood, and props that turn a show sideways — puppets, keyboards, paper bags. He is a Monday regular at The Bomb Comedy Show in Niagara Falls, a co-host of The Green Room podcast, has done roast battles and Trader Jokes joke-swap shows at Spotlight Comedy Club, and performs in virtual reality at Failed To Render VR. Clean, family-friendly sets are available. The full archive is on YouTube at @RyanMorrisComedy.
